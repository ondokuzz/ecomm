# Context Map

## Contexts

| Context | Lives at | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| Identity & Access | Keycloak realm `ecomm` (config in `services/identity-access`) | Customer and Staff authentication, roles and permissions |
| Catalog | `services/catalog` | Products, Variants, categories, specs, pricing |
| Search & Discovery | `services/search-discovery` | Search and faceted filtering of the Catalog: a read projection over Catalog's and Inventory's events, in MongoDB |
| Reviews & Ratings | `services/reviews-ratings` | Reviews and ratings by Customers who paid for what they review, in MongoDB, checked against a projection of Order Management's and Catalog's events |
| Recommendations | `services/recommendations` | "Customers also bought": a projection of Order Management's and Catalog's events in Postgres, joined into Recommendations when asked |
| Cart | `services/cart` | The ephemeral per-Customer Cart |
| Checkout & Pricing | `services/checkout-pricing` | Checkout Sessions: pricing, tax, holding a Cart's Stock, and its Shipping address, validated behind an `AddressValidator` port, and Shipping option; paying one starts the checkout Saga |
| Promotions | `services/promotions` | Coupons and Campaigns |
| Payment | `services/payment` | Gateway integration, authorization/capture/refund, kept as a ledger of Payment transactions |
| Order Management | `services/order-management` | Order lifecycle from placement to delivery, with its Order Status history |
| Inventory | `services/inventory` | Stock levels, Reservations, replenishment, kept as a ledger of Stock movements |
| Fulfillment & Shipping | `services/fulfillment-shipping` | Warehouses, as configuration, Shipping options quoted by Carriers behind its `ShippingProvider` port, and Shipments from pick and pack to the Carrier's delivery, in Postgres |
| Returns & Warranty | `services/returns-warranty` | RMAs, from a Customer's request, checked against the Return Period or the Warranty Window and the units delivered, through Staff's RMA decision, receipt and Inspection, to the returns Saga's resolution, with each RMA's Refund amount, Send-back deadline and RMA history; projections of Fulfillment & Shipping's, Catalog's and Order Management's events, in Postgres |
| Notifications | `services/notifications` | Notifications to Customers, triggered by other contexts' Integration events: a ledger of them in Postgres, each rendered from its Message template and sent through its Notification channel's `NotificationProvider` (email over SMTP; SMS and push to mock providers) as Customers' Notification preferences allow, with a projection of Catalog's Product names |
| AI Support Assistant | `services/ai-support-assistant` | The Support Assistant, grounded in real order/payment/returns data |
| Orchestration | `services/orchestration` | The Sagas — checkout, fulfillment, and returns from Sprint 6 — as workflows on the Temporal server in Compose, which keeps their state in its own Postgres databases; no domain data and no public API of its own |

Not bounded contexts — UI layers over the above:

| App | Lives at |
|---|---|
| Storefront | `frontend/storefront` |
| Admin Console | `frontend/admin-console` |

Browsers reach the services only through the API gateway ([`platform/api-gateway`](./platform/api-gateway/README.md)), which the Storefront's and the Admin Console's nginx send everything under `/api/` to. The gateway routes `/api/<service>/` to Catalog, Inventory, Cart, Checkout & Pricing, Order Management, Payment, Promotions, Search & Discovery, Reviews & Ratings, Recommendations, Fulfillment & Shipping, Returns & Warranty and Notifications, rejects a missing or invalid token at the edge, and never routes internal endpoints; each service still authorizes every request itself ([ADR 0010](./docs/adr/0010-api-gateway-authenticates-at-the-edge.md)).

A request keeps one Correlation ID across every context. It gets one where it enters, unless the caller sent a well-formed one: in the Storefront's or the Admin Console's nginx for a browser, or at the gateway for any other caller. Every service logs it and returns it in problem details, and passes it on every call it makes to another context. Paying hands the Pay request's to the checkout Saga, whose every step carries it, so one request can be followed through the logs, Temporal included. The fulfillment Saga's steps carry the one from the Order event that started it.

## Relationships

> **Since Sprint 4 (#60):** Checkout starts a Checkout Session itself, reserving its Stock through Inventory ([Checkout ADR 0001](./services/checkout-pricing/docs/adr/0001-checkout-sessions-hold-stock-through-reservations.md)), and paying it starts the checkout Saga in Orchestration, which calls the other contexts. In Sprints 1–3 Checkout called Order Management and Payment, and committed the Reservation, itself.

- **Cart → Checkout & Pricing**: Checkout reads the Cart's contents to start a Checkout Session.
- **Cart → (Sprint 8) abandoned-cart recovery**: Cart publishes each Abandoned Cart as a `cart.cart` `ABANDONED` event, with its lines and when it last changed, straight to Kafka rather than through an outbox ([ADR 0002](./docs/adr/0002-ledgers-and-outboxes-not-event-sourcing.md)'s 2026-10-25 note). Its ID is derived from the Customer and that last change, so the same abandonment published again is one a consumer dedupes. Nothing consumes it yet; Sprint 8's recovery will, sending through Notifications.
- **Catalog → Checkout & Pricing**: a Checkout Session prices every line from Catalog's Price when it starts, never from the Cart, and keeps that Price for its lifetime, with the line's Product SKU and Category.
- **Catalog → Search & Discovery**: Search projects Catalog's `catalog.product` and `catalog.category` events, so a Product is searchable, and a Price or a removal shows, a moment after Catalog publishes it. Search keeps the newest version of each and never calls Catalog. The Storefront's listing is Search's ([Search ADR 0001](./services/search-discovery/docs/adr/0001-search-on-mongodb.md)).
- **Inventory → Search & Discovery**: Search projects Inventory's `inventory.stock` events to tell which Products are in Stock; a Variant with no Stock event yet counts as out of Stock.
- **Order Management → Reviews & Ratings**: Reviews projects Order Management's `order-management.order` events to know which Customer bought which Variant, and whether that Order counts: `Paid` or later, and not `Cancelled` or `Returned`. It checks this only when a review is posted, so a review stays if its Order is later cancelled. It keeps the newest version of each Order and never calls Order Management.
- **Catalog → Reviews & Ratings**: Reviews projects Catalog's `catalog.product` events to know each Variant's Product, since an Order names Variants and a review is of a Product. The Storefront's Product cards and pages show Reviews' rating summaries beside Search's and Catalog's data ([Reviews ADR 0001](./services/reviews-ratings/docs/adr/0001-reviews-on-mongodb.md)).
- **Order Management → Recommendations**: Recommendations projects Order Management's `order-management.order` events to know which Variants each Order bought, when it was placed, and whether it counts: `Paid` or later, and not `Cancelled` or `Returned`, the same rule as Reviews. An Order cancelled after it was paid stops counting. It keeps the newest version of each Order and never calls Order Management; its consumer group read the topic from the start, so Orders placed before it existed count.
- **Catalog → Recommendations**: Recommendations projects Catalog's `catalog.product` events to know each Variant's Product, since an Order names Variants and a Recommendation is of a Product, and to leave out Products removed from the Catalog. What was bought together is worked out from both projections when asked, so they may arrive in either order. The Storefront's Product page shows them as "Customers also bought" through the gateway's public `GET /api/recommendations/products/{sku}/also-bought`, with Catalog's data and Reviews' Rating summaries.
- **Catalog → Fulfillment & Shipping**: Fulfillment & Shipping projects Catalog's `catalog.product` events for each Variant's Product name and Serial tracking, keeping the newest version, and a Shipment's lines take them when it is created. A Shipment of a Variant not yet projected is refused for now (503), so the fulfillment Saga tries again.
- **Catalog → Returns & Warranty**: Returns & Warranty projects Catalog's `catalog.product` events for each Variant's Warranty terms and Serial tracking, keeping the newest version. A warranty claim's Warranty Window is worked out from the terms when it is requested and kept on the RMA, so a later change doesn't touch it. A warranty claim on a Variant not yet projected is refused for now (503), for the Customer to try again.
- **Catalog → Promotions**: a Campaign's Categories must be Catalog's, and its amounts in currencies Catalog prices in. Promotions reads Catalog's public Categories and currencies when Staff save a Campaign.
- **Checkout & Pricing → Promotions**: starting a Checkout Session, and applying or removing its Coupon, asks Promotions for every Discount the session's lines are due: the running Campaigns', then the Coupon's, or why the Coupon doesn't apply. Each line goes with its Product's SKU and Category, which decide the Campaigns it gets. Applying a Coupon then holds a Redemption hold for the session until its `expiresAt`, refused as `exhausted` or `alreadyRedeemed` once the Coupon's Usage limits are reached; removing it, or starting a new session, releases the hold. Only Checkout may ask, with its own identity, and the gateway never routes it.
- **Checkout & Pricing → Fulfillment & Shipping**: Fulfillment & Shipping quotes the Shipping options to a destination, in a Currency, from the Warehouse that would ship there, or answers that nothing ships there. Only Checkout may ask, with its own identity, and the gateway never routes it. Checkout asks whenever a Checkout Session is given a Shipping address, and keeps the options quoted.
- **Checkout & Pricing → Inventory**: starting a Checkout Session reserves the Cart's Stock, and replacing one releases its Reservation, with Checkout's own identity.
- **Checkout & Pricing → Orchestration**: paying a Checkout Session starts the checkout Saga through the Temporal client, with the session's ID as the workflow's and the session, with its Shipping address and chosen Shipping option, as its input, and reads how it ended. Checkout calls no other context to pay.
- **Orchestration → Order Management, Payment, Inventory, Promotions, Cart, Checkout & Pricing**: the checkout Saga places the Order with its Shipping, authorizes the Payment for the Order's total, commits the Reservation, marks the Order paid, redeems the session's Coupon (a Coupon redemption, once per session, whatever its Usage limits by then), clears the Cart and ends the session. It compensates a failure by cancelling the Order and voiding an authorized Payment, so a Payment is never left authorized for a cancelled Order. Each step calls the context's command API, with an idempotency key where repeating the command would act twice ([ADR 0009](./docs/adr/0009-sagas-on-temporal.md)); each context owns its own lifecycle.
- **Order Management → Returns & Warranty**: Returns & Warranty projects Order Management's `order-management.order` events (group `returns-warranty.orders`, which read the topic from the start) for each Order's lines' Prices, Discounts and Tax, from which an RMA's Refund amount is worked out when it is requested. An RMA on an Order not yet projected is refused for now (503), for the Customer to try again; RMAs from before the projection gained theirs once it caught up. It keeps the newest version of each Order and never calls Order Management.
- **Order Management → Orchestration**: Orchestration consumes `order-management.order` (group `orchestration.orders`, which read from the latest offset when it first started). A `Paid` Order with a Shipping address starts its fulfillment Saga, `fulfillment:<orderId>`, once however often the event is delivered; an Order paid without one, before Sprint 5, starts nothing and stays `Paid`.
- **Orchestration → Fulfillment & Shipping, Payment, Order Management**: the fulfillment Saga creates the Order's Shipment, captures the Payment once the Shipment is packed, marks the Order `Fulfilled`, dispatches the Shipment, marks the Order `Shipped`, and marks it `Delivered` once the Carrier has delivered it. A declined Capture holds the Shipment and leaves the Order `Paid`. It finds the Payment through Payment's read of an Order's Payments, naming the Customer ([ADR 0009](./docs/adr/0009-sagas-on-temporal.md), revised 2026-10-15).
- **Returns & Warranty → Orchestration, and Orchestration → Returns & Warranty, Payment, Inventory, Fulfillment & Shipping, Order Management**: Orchestration consumes `returns-warranty.rma` (group `orchestration.rmas`, which read the topic from the start, so Sprint 5's approved RMAs gained their Saga). An `APPROVED` RMA starts its returns Saga, `returns:<rmaId>`, once however often the event is delivered, and each later event wakes it. The Saga expires an RMA whose units don't come by its Send-back deadline; once they are inspected, it restocks and refunds an accepted return, reserves, commits and ships a replacement for a confirmed fault (or refunds it with no Stock), closes a refused one, holds the RMA when its Refund is declined, and marks the Order `Returned` once every unit it delivered is refunded. It reads the RMA as Staff do, and calls each context's commands with its own identity.
- **Carrier → Fulfillment & Shipping**: the Carrier reports each Tracking event of a Shipment it booked with a signed Carrier webhook, which the API gateway routes to Fulfillment & Shipping's `POST /webhooks/carrier` without a token. Only the mock carrier sends them so far.
- **Fulfillment & Shipping → Orchestration and Returns & Warranty**: Fulfillment & Shipping publishes a `fulfillment-shipping.shipment` event with each change to a Shipment, for the fulfillment Saga to wait on (Orchestration's group `orchestration.shipments` signals the Order's Saga with each, or a replacement's RMA's returns Saga, and skips one for no running Saga) and Returns & Warranty to check RMAs against. Returns & Warranty projects each Shipment's Order, Customer, Warehouse, lines with their Serial numbers, and when it was delivered (group `returns-warranty.shipments`), takes RMAs only against a delivered one, and sends an approved RMA's units back to its Warehouse.
- **Payment gateway → Payment**: the gateway settles an authorization it left pending, once the Customer's bank confirms it, with a signed Gateway webhook that the API gateway routes to Payment without a token. Only the mock gateway sends them so far.
- **Payment → Notifications, AI Support Assistant**: Payment publishes each change to a Payment, with its Payment transactions, as a `payment.payment` event. Notifications consumes them (group `notifications.payments`, from the latest offset when it first started): an approved Refund records a `PAYMENT_REFUNDED` Notification with its amount and what it was for; a Capture, a declined Payment and a declined Refund record nothing. The Support Assistant (Sprint 7) will consume them too.
- **Fulfillment & Shipping → Notifications**: Notifications consumes `fulfillment-shipping.shipment` (group `notifications.shipments`, from the latest offset when it first started): an `ORDER` Shipment's change to `SHIPPED` records `ORDER_SHIPPED`, with its Carrier and tracking number, and to `DELIVERED` `ORDER_DELIVERED`; a `REPLACEMENT`'s change to `SHIPPED` records `REPLACEMENT_SHIPPED`. Each goes by email, and by SMS and push as the Customer's Notification preferences allow. An `RMA_APPROVED` email gives the Return address of the RMA's Warehouse, which Notifications reads from Fulfillment & Shipping's `GET /warehouses/{id}` with its own `notifications` identity, holding the `NOTIFICATIONS` role.
- **Returns & Warranty → Notifications**: Returns & Warranty publishes each change to an RMA, its request and every later step, as a `returns-warranty.rma` event. Orchestration consumes them (above), and so does Notifications (group `notifications.rmas`, from the latest offset when it first started): an RMA's change to `REQUESTED`, `APPROVED` (with the Return address and Send-back deadline), `REJECTED` (with the reason), `RECEIVED`, `CLOSED` (with the Inspection's note) or `EXPIRED` records the `RMA_` Message type of that name, by email alone. An Inspection, a hold and a backfill record nothing, nor do `REFUNDED` and `EXCHANGED`, which `PAYMENT_REFUNDED` and `REPLACEMENT_SHIPPED` tell.
- **Order Management → Notifications**: Notifications consumes `order-management.order` (group `notifications.orders`, which read from the latest offset when it first started, so nobody is emailed about an Order paid before it existed). An Order's change to `PAID` records an `ORDER_CONFIRMED` Notification by email, once however often the event is delivered, listing the Order's lines and what was paid.
- **Catalog → Notifications**: Notifications projects Catalog's `catalog.product` events (group `notifications.products`, rebuilt by `make notifications-rebuild`) for each Variant's Product name and axis values, keeping the newest version, since an Order's events name only Variants. A line whose Variant isn't projected yet goes by its Variant ID.
- **Identity & Access → Notifications**: Notifications reads each Customer's email address and names from Keycloak's admin API, behind its `CustomerDirectory` port, with its own `notifications` client, whose service account holds realm-management's `view-users`, and keeps them for 10 minutes. It keeps no copy of identity data; a Customer Keycloak doesn't know, or one with no email address, fails their Notification as `noRecipient`.
- **Order Management, Payment, Returns & Warranty → AI Support Assistant**: the assistant queries each context's read APIs to ground its answers in real Customer data.
- **Identity & Access → all contexts**: every service trusts Keycloak-issued JWTs. A Customer's token names the Customer. Checkout and Orchestration each call other contexts with their own client-credentials identity, the `checkout` and `orchestration` clients holding the `CHECKOUT` and `ORCHESTRATION` roles, and each command names the one caller allowed to make it: Checkout reserves and releases Stock, evaluates Discounts and holds and releases Coupons, and Orchestration makes every command the checkout, fulfillment and returns Sagas call ([ADR 0002](./services/identity-access/docs/adr/0002-service-identity-by-client-credentials.md)). Notifications' `notifications` client calls Keycloak's admin API, holding realm-management's `view-users`, and Fulfillment & Shipping's Warehouse read, holding the `NOTIFICATIONS` role.
- **All contexts ↔ Kafka**: the shared backbone for integration events, published from each context's outbox and versioned so consumers keep the newest ([ADR 0002](./docs/adr/0002-ledgers-and-outboxes-not-event-sourcing.md), [ADR 0006](./docs/adr/0006-kafka-as-single-event-backbone.md)). Commands never travel over it.

## Shared vocabulary

Term definitions currently live in one shared [`GLOSSARY.md`](./GLOSSARY.md) at the root rather than per-context files — the vocabulary hasn't diverged per context yet. A context earns its own `GLOSSARY.md` (and this map gets updated to point to it) once its terms need a local definition that differs from the shared one.

## ADRs

System-wide decisions live in [`docs/adr/`](./docs/adr/). Context-specific decisions live inside the context they belong to (e.g. [`services/catalog/docs/adr/`](./services/catalog/docs/adr/), [`services/checkout-pricing/docs/adr/`](./services/checkout-pricing/docs/adr/)).
